Local video editing

Make a short video, your way.

MakeShort is a desktop video editor for arranging clips, images, captions, and locally generated voiceovers on a timeline, then exporting a vertical video.

View the open-source project How your data is handled

Edit locally

Projects and media are saved on the computer running MakeShort. The app does not provide a hosted editing or account service.

Compose a timeline

Arrange video, image, text, captions, and voiceover layers, preview the composition, and export an MP4.

Connect platforms

Users can choose to connect YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok accounts and send a finished video to a selected platform.

Use of third-party platforms

You control whether to connect an account or upload a video. Platform access and publishing are governed by the relevant platform's terms, permissions, and account settings. MakeShort is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, YouTube, Meta, Instagram, or TikTok.
